All Study Spaces

Gelman Library

Location: Foggy Bottom Campus, Main Library

Contact: (202) 555-0101

Gelman Library is the main academic library on GW’s Foggy Bottom campus and offers a balanced mix of quiet study zones, individual desks, and collaborative rooms. The building spans multiple floors with reliable Wi-Fi, plenty of outlets, and a variety of seating styles. It’s a popular choice for focused study sessions, research work, or group assignments between classes.

Duquès Building

Location: School of Business, student lounge & open seating areas

Contact: (202) 555-0102

Duques Building, home to the GW School of Business, provides bright study lounges and open seating areas ideal for small group discussions or quick work between lectures. With comfortable seating, strong Wi-Fi, and natural lighting throughout the building, it’s a convenient and welcoming space for students who prefer a more social study environment.

Himmelfarb Library

Location: FHealth Sciences Library, near GW Hospital

Contact: (202) 555-0103

Himmelfarb Library serves GW’s health sciences programs and offers a quieter, more focused study setting. With computer workstations, individual carrels, and access to academic resources, it’s especially suitable for students working on intensive reading, exam prep, or long research sessions near the medical campus.
