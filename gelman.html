About this Place

Location & Hours

Location

Located at the center of GW’s Foggy Bottom campus, within walking distance of academic buildings, residence halls, and the Metro station.

Hours

Open for extended hours during the semester, with early morning and late-night access. Specific floor schedules may vary each term.
