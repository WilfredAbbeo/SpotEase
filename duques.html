Duquès Building

Home of the GW School of Business. A highly active building with classrooms, breakout rooms, and multiple open study spaces. Many rooms are available for student use when classes are not in session.

Business School Hub Many Classrooms Breakout Rooms Open Lounges Great for Team Projects Walk-in Classroom Study Whiteboards Available Projector-Equipped Rooms Plenty of Outlets Near Campus Cafés

About this Place

Study Environment

Duquès Hall offers one of the most flexible study environments on campus. Classrooms become quiet study spaces when not in active use, and breakout rooms are ideal for group projects. Open lounges on multiple floors provide casual areas for laptop work or quick meetings.

Best For

  • Group projects needing breakout rooms
  • Quick study sessions between classes
  • Casual laptop work in open lounge spaces
  • Classroom study when no class is scheduled
