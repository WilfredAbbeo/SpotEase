About this Place

Quiet & Silent Study Areas

Himmelfarb’s upper floors are organized very deliberately by noise level. The second floor is a true silent floor reserved for graduate and advanced degree students, with individual carrels and low-distraction seating meant for deep focus and board-style exam prep. The third floor is a quiet study floor, offering a mix of open and closed carrels and tables for students who want a calm environment with minimal background noise.

Group & Collaboration Spaces

The first floor is more active and allows quiet conversation, with shared tables, lounge-style seating, and rolling whiteboards that work well for group review or case discussions. Group study rooms on multiple floors can be reserved in advance for small teams; most are equipped with large screens and whiteboards so health sciences students can work through problem sets, clinical cases, or presentations together without leaving the library.

Technology & Specialized Learning Tools

Technology-focused areas on the third floor, including the Bloedorn Technology Center, provide access to public computers, printers, statistical software, and interactive tools like the BodyViz 3D anatomy workstation. These spaces make it easier to move between reading, data analysis, and hands-on exploration of anatomical content in a single study session.

Healthy Living & Wellness Support

Himmelfarb also supports well-being through the Healthy Living @ Himmelfarb initiative and the Healthy Living Collection on the first floor. Students can find materials on stress management and mental health alongside puzzles, board games, yoga mats, and other light activity resources, offering short, low-pressure breaks from intensive study without leaving the building.

Best For

  • Intensive reading and exam preparation for medicine, public health, and nursing coursework.
  • Students who need clearly separated silent, quiet, and collaborative zones to match different study tasks.
  • Small health sciences study groups working through cases, lab material, or problem-based learning activities.
  • Sessions that combine library research with computer work, statistical software, or 3D anatomy tools.
  • Students who want a study space that also offers short, on-site options to reset and manage stress in between study blocks.

Location & Hours

Location

Located near GW Hospital and the health sciences buildings on the Foggy Bottom campus, within walking distance of clinical and classroom facilities.

Hours

Open for extended hours during the semester to support health sciences students with varying schedules. Specific floor and service hours may vary by term, so students typically check the library’s official site for the latest details.
